- (h) he  has  not  been  ordered  to  give  security  for  good  behaviour under the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973;
- (i) he has not been dismissed from the Army, Navy or Air Force or any Reserve Forces of India;
- (j) he has not more than one wife living;

Provided that-

- the Central Government may relax the conditions specified in clause(d) in respect of any establishments;
- a person who is ineligible by virtue of the provisions in clause (g)  or  clause  (h)  may  be  enrolled  if  he  produces  from  the Government of the State of which he is a resident, a certificate that he is fir to be enrolled.
- the Central Government may, for special reasons, exempt any person from the operation of clause(j).

## 11. Verification .

- An  enrolling  officer  shall  satisfy  himself  in  the  manner  laid  down  by  the Government, that the applicarion for enrolment vide schedule I, is in order and that the applicant is eligible under regulation 10.
- The  antecedents  of  the  person  found  fir  for  grant  of  Commission  or  for enrolment in the Service shall be verified from the Police authorities of his area.
- Medical Examination. A  person  found fit  for  grant of  Commission or for enrolment shall be required to present himself for medical examination at a time and place to be specified in this behalf.
- Rejection. A  person  rejected  on  medical  grounds  for  grant  of  commission  or  for enrolment in the service may reappear for the medical examination and shall be governed by the same regulations as for the regular Navy.

## 14. Appointment.

- A person enrolled in the service shall as for as possible be posted to the nearest Naval establishment from his hometown.
- The rules for the appointment of Officers to the Service shall be the same as for the regular Navy
