- Every  person  enrolled  shall  be  administrated  an  oath  or  affirmation  by  his Commanding Officer, as is done for the regular Naval personnel vide schedule II.
- An  entry  to  the  effect  that  the  person  enrolled  has  taken  oath  or  affirmation shall be endorsed in the enrolment form and shall be signed by him and authenticated by the signature of his Commanding Officer.
