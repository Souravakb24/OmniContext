- (b)    is regular in attending training periods as laid down from time to time:
- ©    is recommended by the Commanding Officer:
- (d)    has qualified in case of promotion from Sub-Lieutenant to Lieutenant.
- Substantive Lieutenant Commanders with six to twelve years seniority in that rank shall be eligible for promotion to the rank of Commander on the basis of selection.
- Counting of previous Service. Previous full time Commission in the Regular Forces or previous  commissioned  service in  the  Army  Reserve,  Territorial  Army  and  Indian  Air  Force Auxiliary Service shall be counted for the purpose of promotion.
