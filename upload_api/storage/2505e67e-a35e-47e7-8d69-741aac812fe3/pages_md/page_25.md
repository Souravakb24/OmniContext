
NoteOn joining the Service officers shall be allowed to count for the purpose of increment of Pay-

- (a) previous full paid commissioned service in the regular Armed Forces;
- (b) previous  called up or  embodied  service  in  full  and one-fourth  of  other service in-
- (i) Army Reserves-

Class X, A, B and S categories of Reserve Officers of the Army;

- (ii) Navy Reserves-

Reserves  comprising  retired/released  regular,  Short  Service  and  Emergency Commissioned Officer.

- (iii) Air Force Reserves-

Reserves  comprising  retired/released  regular,  Short  Service  and  Emergency Commissioned Officers.  Air Defence Officers, Authority Air Force Officers.


## CHAPTER III


## SAILORS-RECRUITMENT, PROMOTION, TRANSFER, DISCHARGES AND RETIREMENTS

- Recruitment of Sailors. Enrolment of sailors fro all branches of the Service shall be carried out through the recruiting organizations, or by the Commanding  Officers of establishments or by the Officer-in-Charge of Coast Batteries or any other authority nominated by Naval Headquarters as the case may be.
- Application  for  Enrolment. (1)  Application  for  enrolment  shall  be  made  in  the enrolment  form  to  the  nearest  recruiting  organization,  or  Commanding  Officer  of  any establishment or Officer-in-Charge of Coast Batteries.
