- The officer to whom an application under sub-regulation (1)is made shall cause the application  to  fill  up  and  sign  in  his  presence  a  statement  set  forth  in  the  enrolment  form prescribed in this behalf.
- An  applicant  shall be  required  to produce  a certificate  from  his employer,  if any permitting him to join the Service and giving an undertaking that the applicant shall be  made available  for  training  and  embodiment  as  and  when  required  by  the  Administrative  authority concerned.
- In peacetime, the period training shall  be  adjusted  to suit  the convenience of the enrolled person as far as possible.
- If the applicant is accepted for enrolment, he shall be required to sign a declaration provided at the foot of the enrolment form referred to in sub-regulation (2)
- The  Recruiting  Officer,  when  he  is  satisfied  that  the  applicant  understands  the questions put to him and consents to the conditions of the Service, shall sign a certificate to that effect on the said enrolment form and the applicant shall thereupon be deemed to be enrolled.
- Age of Enrolment. In order to be eligible for enrolment a candidate shall not be below twenty-one years of age and shall not be more than forty years of age.

Provided  that  the upper  age  limit  may  be  relaxed  in  the  case  of  specially  experienced candidates at the discretion of the Chief of the Naval.

- Period for Enrollment. A sailor shall be enrolled for a further period of seven years, which may further be extended for a period not exceeding two years at a time, provided that the total period so extended shall not exceed fifteen years.
- Educational and Technical Standards. The educational and technical standards of the candidates  shall  be  such  as  may  be  laid  down  by  the  Government  from  time  for  the  various branches.
- Promotion. Promotion shall be governed by the instructions laid down from time to time by the Government
- Transfer. A person enrolled in the Service shall be transferred by the officer who enrolled him to establishment of the Service constituted for the zone in which such person for the time resides.
- Allotment of Personal Numbers. (1) Every person shall, on enrolment, in the Service, be assigned a personal number.
- Discharge. (1)  When a sailor, while undergoing initial training is found to be suffering from  a  disability  which  is  likely  to  prevent  him  becoming  an  efficient  sailor,  he  shall  be medically examined, and if found unfit, discharge from the Service.
