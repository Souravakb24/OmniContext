
Schedule VII shall be issued to sailors whenever they report for annual or voluntary training or for camp and shall be recovered from sailors on conclusion of the training or camp.

- Application  of  Clothing  Regulations. The  provisions  of  Clothing  Regulations  for sailors contained in Instructions relating to Supply of Victualling, Clothing and Mess Traps to IN Ships etc. (INBR 13) and Uniform Regulations for sailors in the Indian Navy (INBR 3) and in other orders issued from time to time shall apply to the personnel of the Service except where otherwise stated in these regulations.
- Provisioning of Clothing. (1) Ready-made garments stocked in Clothing Stores are to be issued and any alterations to the garments so issued that may be considered necessary by the Commanding Officer of the establishment shall be carried out by the Clothing Stores.
- Material  for making garments shall be issued when garments of the requisits size are not  available in  the  Clothing Stores, the quantity  of  cloth  and garniture material to be issued being restricted to the scales laid down in the Clothing Regulations.
- Where the material for making garments is issued under sub-regulation (1) the cost of the stitching shall be borne by Government.
- Maintenance of Clothing Accounts. (1) Clothing accounts shall be maintained by the Base Logistics Officer or the Supply Officer concerned, as the case may be.
- Separate clothing legers for the Service shall be maintained in accordance with the current regulations.
- Replacement of Outgrown Garments. (1) Where the garments are out grown by the wearer  and  the Commanding Officer is  satisfied  that  the  issue  of  new  garments  has  become necessary  and  that  due  attention  was  paid  to  the  instructions  laid  down  for  fitting  he  may authorize replacement of garments free of charge.
- The life of the garments withdrawn from the sailors shall be determined by a Board of Survey to be appointed locally by the Commanding Officer and noted in the clothing ledger when re-issuing the garments.
- Garments which are replaced under sub-regulation (1) shall be withdrawn from the sailors, washed if necessary, and taken on charge for re-issue.
- Payment issues. Sailors are allowed to purchase on payment such item of clothing and necessaries as are included in Schedule VI and VIII.
- Stock  of  Boots. (1)  Twenty  per  cent  of  requirement  of  boots  over  and  above  the authorized proportion may be maintained.
- The Commanding Officers shall work out the estimates of the requirements of boots on the strength of their establishments on 1 st April of each year.
