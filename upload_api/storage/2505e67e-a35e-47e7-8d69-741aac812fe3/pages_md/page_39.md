- Any  person  belonging  to  an  establishment  may  be  attached  by  the  Prescribed Authority  at  his  own  request  or  otherwise  to  any  establishment  of  the  Service  or  to  any establishment of the Indian Navy.
- Any  person belonging  to  an  establishment,  who  leaves  his  residence  for  the  time being  and  thereby  leaves  the  area  in  which  the  establishment  wherein    he  is  serving  is constituted shall, if he does not intend to return to that area, notify the Prescribed Authority of that area of his change of residence.
- Where a person mentioned in sub-regulation (6) intends to return but does not in fact return to his area within three months of his departure, he shall immediately on the expiry of the said period send intimation in writing to the Prescribed Authority.
