## SECTION I - TRAINING

- T raining  Directives. Training  Directives  shall  be  issued  by  the  Naval  Headquarters from time to time.
- Responsibility  of  training  and  arrangement  for  Training  Classes. The  Director  of Naval Training shall be responsible for arranging training and such classes as may be necessary at the training centers in all the establishments of the Service.
