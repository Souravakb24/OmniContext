- Annual Training Grants. Annual training grants shall be admissible to all ships and establishments.  The rules for drawing the amount and the accounting procedure for budgeting shall be the same as for the establishment of the regular Navy.
- Cook Houses, Latrines and Similar Structures. Expenditure is authorized on account of erection and removal of cookhouses, latrines and similar structures when free teenage, where authorized, is not available.  Expenditure is also authorized in connection with the erection and removal  of  chulas,  partitions  and  similar  structures  required  to  be  constructed  within  free teenage authorized.   The  actual cost  shall be sanctioned by  the  Administrative Authority and debited to the expenses of the Defence Services Funds.
- Transport Charges. Occasional expenditure on account of the transport  of clothing, equipment  and  stores,  provided  Government  transport  is  not  available,  shall  be  authorized. Transportation shall be authorized by the Administrative Authority and the Cost thereof debited to transportation charges of the Service.
