- Application of  the Act and Regulations, All persons enrolled  in the Service  shall be subject to the Navy Act, 1957 (62 of 1957) and these regulations.
- Application of Naval Regulations ,    Where no provision or insufficient provision has been made in these regulations in relation to any matter for which regulations have been made for  the  Indian  Navy, the Regulations for the Navy  in so far  as  they are not  inconsistent  with these regulations, shall apply to such matter.
- Constitution of Establishments .  The Central Government may constitute such number such number of establishments of the Service as it thinks fir and may disband or reconstitute any such establishments.
- Composition of Indian Naval Auxiliary Service Cadre . The Cadre of the Service shall consist of-
- (a) officers; and
- (b) sailors.
- Observance and enforcement of the Regulations. An officer of the Service shall    acquaint himself with and shall enforce the provisions of the Act, the     regulations made there  under and  all orders  issued  by  the  Government,  the  Chief  of  the Naval  Staff, Administrative authority or his superior officer, and shall observe the established customs and practices of the Indian Navy.

## 9. Promulgation of Orders.

- The  Commanding  Officer  shall  ensure  that  any  new  orders  concerning  the officers  and  sailors  working  in  his  establishment  are  read  to  them  by  the  Divisional Officers and  copies  thereof  kept  at a place  accessible  to such personnel  for  at least a week after their promulgation.
- When  orders  referred  to  in  sub-regulation  (1)  affect  particular  duties,  the Commanding  Officer  shall  ensure  that  the  personnel  so  affected  are  thoroughly instructed as to their contents.
- Eligibility for Recruitment.
- In order to be eligible for entry to the Service, a person shall be-
- (a) a citizen of India, or
- (b)     a subject of Bhutan, or
- (c)     a subject of Nepal, or
- (d)       a person of Indian origin who has migrated from any country with the intention of    permanently settling in India.
