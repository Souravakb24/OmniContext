## NOTES

- Separate records on this form are to be maintained in respect of (a) Sailors (including sea- going Boys) (b) Artificer Apprentices and (c) Boys under training.
- All punishments inflicted on board the Ship including warrant punishments but excluding minor punishments awarded under sub-regulations (5), (6), (7) and (8) of regulation 21, shall be entered on date of award consecutively irrespective of the ranks of offenders.
- This record is to be inspected and signed by the Commanding Officer weekly.
- To  be  closed  on  the  last  day  of  each  Quarter  and  forwarded  with  the  appropriate punishment return.
- Alphabetical category of offences to be noted in column 7 of this form shall correspond to the various categories specified in the Punishment Return.
