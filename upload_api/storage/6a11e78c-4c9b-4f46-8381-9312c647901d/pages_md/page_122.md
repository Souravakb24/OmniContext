
## FORM No.9


## WARRANT  ORDERING  A  COURT-MARTIAL  (TO  BE  USED  BY  CONVENING AUTHORITY HOLDING A COMMISSION FROM THE CHIEF OF THE NAVAL STAFF TO ORDER A COURT MARTIAL


(See Regulation 159)


By Whereas  ……………………………  of  Indian  Naval  Ship………………………..has transmitted to me a letter alleging the misconduct of……………………………..(hereinafter referred to as the accused) and whereas I think fit that the accused shall be tried by court-martial, I do hereby in exercise of the powers conferred on me by omission from the Chief of the Naval Staff order a court-martial to be held for the trial of the accused on the charges listed in the accompanying charge-sheet and nominate you as president thereof.
