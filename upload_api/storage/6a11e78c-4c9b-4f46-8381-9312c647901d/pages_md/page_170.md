
(d)  When the court has decided on the sentence the trial judge advocate shall draw up the sentence, which is to be signed by every member of the court notwithstanding any difference of opinion that there may have been among the members.  The sentence shall be counter-signed by the trial judge advocate.

- (a)  The  court  shall  then  re-assemble,  the  accused  shall  be  brought  in  and  the defending officer, the prosecutor and audience and witnesses admitted.  The trial judge advocate shall then announce the sentence.
- (b) The court shall assemble for this purpose with their caps on.
- After the sentence has been announced, the president shall direct that the accused be  removed  and  declare  the  court  to  be  dissolved  and  the  National  Flag  to  be  hauled down.
- The president shall wait on the convening authority or the senior officer present with the letter reporting the finding and the sentence.
