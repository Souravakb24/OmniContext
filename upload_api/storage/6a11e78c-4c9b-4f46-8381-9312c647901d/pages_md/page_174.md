- Where a person subject to Military, Naval, Air Force or Coast Guard Law, or any other  law  relating  to  the  Armed  Forces  of  the  Union  for  the  time  being  in  force  has committed  an  offence  which  in  the  opinion  of  competent  Military,  Naval,  Air  Force  or Coast Guard authority, as the case may be, ought to be tried by a Magistrate in accordance with the civil law in force or where the Central Government has on a reference mentioned in rule8, decided that proceedings against such person be instituted before a Magistrate, the Commanding Officer of such person shall after giving a written notice to  the Magistrate concerned, deliver such person under proper escort to that Magistrate.
