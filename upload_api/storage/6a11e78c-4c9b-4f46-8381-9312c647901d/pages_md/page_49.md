- Dismissal after confinement: When a sailor has been dismissed or discharged from the Service, notice thereof shall be given by the Commanding Officer to the authorities at the place of confinement to which he is sent in order that he may be set at liberty at the expiration of his sentence.
- Escorts: -(1) A  sufficient  escort  shall  accompany  each  offender  sent  to confinement.

(2)  The commanding Officer of a ship on whose books a sailor is borne on the date of release shall make arrangements for some proper person or persons to be at the place of confinement at the exact time when the sailor may be entitled to his release, to receive and conduct the offender on board; when the sentence expires on a Sunday the offender is entitled to be released on the preceding Saturday.


## SECTION IV - CIVIL AUTHORITY

- 114A.  Persons subject to the Act charged before Magistrate: The rules regarding the  procedure  to be  followed  in  cases  where  a  person  subject  to  Naval  Law  is brought before a Magistrate charged with an offence for which he is liable to be tried by a court-martial shall be as in Appendix IV;
- Arrest  by  civil  authority:  (1)  The  Commanding  Officer  shall  afford  every facility  to  the  civil  authority  in  detecting  and  apprehending  persons  serving  on board whose arrest is  required on  any  criminal charge, but he shall require  any police officer, or other civil officer coming on board to search for or apprehend such  persons,  to  produce  his  warrant  or  to  show  satisfactory  evidence  of  the capacity in which he acts.

(2) No officer or sailor in Naval custody shall be claimed by the civil authorities for  trial  in  a  civil  court,  except  on  a  direction  of  the  nature  of  Habeas  Corpus made by a High Court or under an order by the Government in this behalf.


(3)  Such officer or sailor may, however, be surrendered voluntarily to answer a criminal charge if such charge is in respect of a civil offence more serious than the Naval Offence for which he is held in Naval custody;


Provided  that  the  previous  approval  of  the  Administrative  Authority concerned shall, wherever possible, be obtained before such a course is taken.
