- (e) Planning,  execution  and  record  of  modifications  and  Alterations and Additions (As and As) to weapon systems.
- (f) Planning and conduct of refits, testing and tunning and alignment of weapon systems.
- (g) Liaison  with  dockyards,  Base  Maintenance  Units  (BMU),  Fleet Maintenance  Units  (FMU),  Weapon  Equipment  Depots  (WED), Materials Organisations, Missile Base, Research and Development Laboratories etc.

Such inspection should normally be carried out in the presence of the relieving officers.


0627.  Distribution  of  Responsibility. Depending  on  the  density  of  the weapons systems and the size of the ships, the responsibility of Weapon Maintenance officer may be distributed to more than one Electrical officer.


0628.  When no Weapon Maintenance Officer Borne. In  case  there  is  no officer of the electrical branch borne, such duties may be assigned by the Captain to any officer of the Executive branch.
