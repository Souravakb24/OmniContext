## 0120. Acceptance of fees for Private Music Engagements by an I.N. Band or any member thereof or Ship's Company.


(1) Private  Engagements  by  I.N.Band. Indian  Navy  Bands  when  permitted to accept  'Private  Engagements'  shall  charge fees to the organisation or  person requesting the Band's services. The Band performers may accept their share, out of the  fees  charged  by  the  Band,  in  accordance  with  the  rules  and  scale  fixed  by  the Chief of the Naval Staff.


(2) Private  Engagements  by  Individual  Members  of  the  Band  or Ship's Company. Members of an I.N. Band or Ship's company when permitted to accept a private music engagement, either as  individuals or as a group, may accept fees.
