## SECTION I - ORGANISATION


0901. Education Branch. (1) The Education Branch of the Navy consists of commissioned officers.

- The organisation is as under: -
- (a) A Directorate of Naval Education at Naval Headquarters un der the Director of  Naval  Education  who  is  assisted  by  a  Joint  Director, Deputy Directors and Assistant Directors.
- (b) A  Directorate  of  Naval  Oceanology  and  Meteorology  at  Naval Headquarters under the Director of Oceanology  and  Meteorology for professional duties in the field of Oceanology and Meteorology.
- (c) Command  Education  officers  holding  Staff  appointments  under Administrative Authorities.
- (d) Command Met officers/Staff Met officers holding staff appointments under Administrative Authorities/Senior Officer.
