- (h) The  custody  of  Confidential  Books  of  Reference,  valuable  stores and such portable fittings of the gunnery system as are classified as stores but do not come  under the charge of the  Weapons Maintenance  officer,  Electrical  officer,  (SDAE),  nominated  Air Engineer  officer,  or  the  officers  borne  for  explosive  accounting duties.
- He shall be guided by: -
- (a) Firing Manuals.
- (b) Handbooks of Equipments.
- (c) Drill Books.
- (d) Fleet, Flotilla and/or command Orders.

1413. When borne/nominated for Explosive Accounting Duties. (1)  He  is responsible to the Gunnery officer for ship's gunnery weapons as follows: -

- (a) For  the  execution  of  his  duties  generally  and  for  the  particular observance of the various instructions given in the Naval Magazine and Explosives Regulations and the Naval Cordite Regulations.
- (b) For the custody and replenishment of naval armament stores.
- (c) For the drill at and firing of saluting guns.
- (d) For ensuring that: -
- The munitions in his charge are kept in good order and are properly expended.
- The  full  quantity  of  munitions  authorised  for  his charge is on board.
- A proper economy is exercised in the consumption of naval armament stores.
- The necessary books and documents relating to the account of munitions are kept up-to-date.
