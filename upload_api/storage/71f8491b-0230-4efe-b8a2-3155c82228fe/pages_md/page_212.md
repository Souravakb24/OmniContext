1803. P.T. Officer. (1) When an officer is detailed to carry out the duties of the P.T. officer, he shall conduct physical training in accordance with Handbooks of Physical Training in force and shall assist in the provision of games and sports for officers and sailors. He shall advise the Captain and Divisional officers on the physical well-being of the sailors, and on welfare matters.


(2) He shall supervise the swimming instructions and shall constantly examine the record of instructions in swimming.


1804.  Medical  Officers. Medical  officers  appointed  for  physical  training duties  shall  carry  out  such  advisory  and  other  duties  as  may  be  required  by  the officers to whose Commands they are attached.
