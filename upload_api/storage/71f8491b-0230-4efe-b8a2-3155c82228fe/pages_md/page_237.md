## SECTION I - GENERAL


2201. Meteorological  and  Oceanological  Services.  (1)  The  Object  of the  Meteorological  and  Oceanological  services  of  the  Indian  Navy  is  to  provide necessary Meteorological and Oceanological information, warnings and outputs for: -

- (a)  Efficient  planning  and  execution  of  Naval  operations  including  air  and submarine operations.
- (b) Safety of aircraft, ships and submarines.
- (c) Strategic and tactical exploitation of environmental parameters for Naval exercises and operations.
- The services will also be provided  for civil aircraft/vessels  of  the  Indian Union  and  foreign  (civil)  and  military  aircraft/vessels  operating  from  and  through Indian Naval airfields/ports/harbours.
