- (b) The  above  benefits  may  be  sanctioned  after  observing  the following formalities: -

## SECTION II - OFFICERS


2510. Confidential reports on Officers. (1)  Confidential Reports on Naval officers  shall  be  rendered  on  forms  as  prescribed  and  as  per  general  instructions issued by the Chief of the Naval Staff.


(2) All reports are to be rendered in original only. There should be no erasures or  overwriting  of  numerical  assessments.  If  amendment  is  to  be  made,  the  old assessment should be crossed through and new assessment entered and signed in full. No copies shall be made or retained by any authority. Extracts of relevant portion of confidential Reports may however be made under the following circumstances: -

- (a) For communicating adverse remarks to the officer by post.
- (b) When an officer reported upon feels aggrieved by the adverse comments which are communicated to him under the provisions of this regulation and requests for a copy of relevant extracts.
- Confidential Reports are of the following types: -
- (a) Annual Confidential Report. (ACR).
- (b) Early Annual Confidential Report. (Early ACR).
