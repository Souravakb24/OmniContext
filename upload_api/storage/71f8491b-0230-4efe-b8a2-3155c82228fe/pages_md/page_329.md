- (c) Administrative  Authorities  are  hereby  empowered  inter  alia  to engage a counsel to conduct the following proceedings in a court of law, after a case has been referred for arbitration: -
- (i) For securing implementation of the award of an arbitrator.
- (ii) To challenge the award of an arbitrator.
- (iii) When  the  matter  is  referred  by  an  arbitrator  for  the opinion of the court.
- (d) The  proceedings  before  an  arbitrator  not  being  proceedings  in  a court of law, Administrative Authorities are not to employ a lawyer to conduct a case  on behalf of the  Government,  before  an arbitrator, without the previous approval of the Chief of the Naval Staff.
- A  list  of  civil  law  officers,  and  the  Naval  officers  who  are  entitled  to  consult them, is  given  in regulation 3311.
