
## 8. Intensity of lights


(a) The minimum luminous intensity of lights shall be calculated by using the formula.


$$
I = 3 . 4 3 \ X \ 1 0 ^ { 6 } \ X \ T \ X \ D ^ { 2 } \ X \ K ^ { \cdot D }
$$


Where I is luminous intensity in candelas under service conditions,

T is threshold factor 2 X 10 lux, D is range of visibility (luminous range) of the light in nautical miles, K is atmospheric transmissivity.


For prescribed lights the value of K shll be 0.8, corresponding to meteorological visibility of approximately 13 nautical miles.
