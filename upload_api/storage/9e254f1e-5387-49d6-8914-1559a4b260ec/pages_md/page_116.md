## 5.23 Re-Tendering

- 5.23.1 Re-tendering may be recommended by the PC/ CNC and approved by  the  CFA  with  the  concurrence  of  Integrated  Finance,  where original approval was accorded with the concurrence of Integrated Finance, after recording the reasons/ proper justification in writing. In cases where nil bids are received against a tender, re-tendering may be ordered by the CFA without IFA concurrence, only if there are no changes in the mode of tendering/ specifications/ RFP , etc., to give another opportunity to interested vendors and in anticipation of better vendor response. Re-tendering may also be considered under the following circumstances:-
- (a) Offer(s) do not conform to qualitative requirements and other terms and conditions set out in the RFP .
