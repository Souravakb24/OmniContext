## 6.22 Access to Books of Accounts

- 6.22.1 Access  to  Documents/  Information . If it  is  found  to  the satisfaction of the Buyer, that the Seller has engaged an agent or paid commission or influenced any person to obtain the contract as described above, the Seller on a specific request of the Buyer will be required to provide necessary information and inspection of the relevant documents/ information.

## 6.23 Patents and Other Industrial Property Rights

- 6.23.1   Quoted Prices to be Inclusive of Charges on Account of Copyright, etc .  The prices stated in the contract shall be deemed to include all amounts payable for the use of patents, copyright, registration charges, trademarks and any other industrial property rights.
- 6.23.2   Indemnity .   The Seller shall indemnify the Buyer against all claims, including claim by any third party at any time on account of the infringement  of  any  or  all  the  rights  mentioned  in  Paragraph 6.23.1, whether such claims arise in respect of manufacture or use.  It  shall  be  the  responsibility  of  the  Seller  to  complete  the supplies,  irrespective  of  the  fact  of  infringement  of  any  such rights.

## 6.24 Non-Disclosure of Contractual Documents/ Information

- 6.24.1   Non-Disclosure of Contract Information .  Except with the written consent of the Buyer/ Seller, either party shall not disclose the contract  or  any  provision,  specification,  plan,  design,  pattern, sample or information thereof to any third party, unless required by either Government of the parties or by the Seller's suppliers solely  for  the  purpose  of  performing  the  obligations  under  the contract.
- 6.24.2   Disclosure in Confidence .   Any disclosure to any person permitted under the above clause shall be made in confidence, and shall extend only so far as may be necessary for the purposes of the contract.
