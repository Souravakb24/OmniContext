- 7.1.5 As mentioned above, while all concerned may take an in-principle call that there will be no import of Defence equipment going forward, certain Defence items, such as spares and equipment support from foreign OEMs/ suppliers, may need to be obtained through imports, until  they are indigenised and become available indigenously. Till then, imports would be required to ensure serviceability of existing imported assets at all times, and also to ensure value for money spent. In those cases where indigenous development/ production is not feasible, a categorical exception will have to be obtained from Raksha Mantri or any other authority as stipulated by MoD from time to time, to undertake ex-import procurement. For undertaking any such Defence imports, after complying with the restrictions/ directions  mentioned  above,  it  is  of  paramount  importance  to lay  down  comprehensive  procedures  and  policies  in  respect  of foreign procurements in line with MoD's procedures/ policies, as well as international procurement practices, for implementation by all  concerned. While all proposals for procurement of goods and services are to be processed as per the procedures laid down in other  Chapters  of  this  Manual,  some  special  features  relating  to foreign  procurements  are  explained  in  this  Chapter.  Paragraph 2.1.10 (b) (Chapter 2) is also relevant.
