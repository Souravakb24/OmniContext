- (b)  Under  QCBS  selection,  the  technical  proposals  may  be allotted  a  weightage  of  70%,  with  the  financial  proposals being allotted a weightage of 30%, or any other respective weightages as declared  in  the  RFP  (e.g.  60:40,  50:50,  but not greater than 80% for technical proposals). The proposed weightages for quality and cost shall be specified in the RFP .
- (i) Using  the  technical  scores  for  each  proposal  given  by the  CEC,  and  the  price  bids  after  they  are  opened,  an Evaluated Bid Score of the bid under consideration, will be calculated for each responsive/ qualified bid, covering both the technical scores and financial bids by weighing them with the prespecified weightages, and then adding them up.
