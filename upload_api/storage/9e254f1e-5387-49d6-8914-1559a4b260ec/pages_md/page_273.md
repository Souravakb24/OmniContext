
## 12.15 Monitoring the Contract

- 12.15.1 Before commencement of the services, the service provider/ contractor  shall  submit  to  the  Procuring  Entity  for  approval, a  programme  showing  the  general  methods,  arrangements, order and timing for all activities in accordance with Paragraph 12.14.1. The services shall be carried out in accordance with the approved programme as updated. CFA shall nominate an officer/ committee to be involved throughout in the conduct of the contract, and to continuously monitor the performance of the service provider/contractor.

## 12.16 Aspects not Covered

- 12.16.1 Any  aspect  not  covered  in  this  Chapter,  will  be  as  per  the relevant provisions in other Chapters of this Manual.
