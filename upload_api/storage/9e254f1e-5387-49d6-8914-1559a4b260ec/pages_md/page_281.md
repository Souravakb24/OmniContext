- 13.7.1 Criterion for Determination of L1 .  The L1 Shipyard/ Firm shall be determined as follows:-

In  case  of  ICG,  since  NA  spares  are  not  part  of  Refit/ Repair Package, determination of L1 bidder is to be considered on account of cost of Refit/Repairs inclusive of Services and mandatory  components/refurbishment  items  for  propulsion and power generation, which are to be included upfront in the RFP.
