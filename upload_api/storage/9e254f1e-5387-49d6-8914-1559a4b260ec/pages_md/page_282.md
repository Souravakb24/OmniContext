- 13.7.3 Negotiations for 'Not Quoted' DLs .  Having loaded the highest cost for the 'Not Quoted' DLs, the PC/ CNC should subsequently engage with the L1 bidder to match the lowest available prices, to ensure that reasonable price is paid for the 'Not Quoted' DLs in the final contract price.
- 13.7.4  Quality and Cost Based Selection (QCBS). Wherever required, QCBS  may  be  used  for  evaluation.  The  procedure  for  QCBS shall be as per Sub Paragraph 5.31.6 (b) (Chapter 5). Following technical  parameters  may,  inter  alia,  form  the  criteria  for evaluation of bidders under QCBS:-
- Dry dock scheduling.
- Quality of infrastructure.
- Refit/Repairs Planning.
- QA/ QC.
- Overseeing and monitoring.
- Material/ equipment handling and operational logistics.
- Management practices and governance.
- Human resource capabilities and workforce development.
- Safety standards and services.
- Past experience of refits/Repairs.
- Timely completion of past contracts.
- Subcontractor/ vendor base.
- Proximity to Naval establishment.
- Customer reviews.
- Administrative arrangements.
- 13.7.5 Evaluation criteria shall be stated upfront in the RFP .

## 13.8 Format of Contract
