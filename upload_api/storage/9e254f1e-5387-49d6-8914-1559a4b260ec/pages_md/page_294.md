- 14.9.7  Invocation of Guarantees .    Guarantees can only be invoked after fulfilling the following conditions:-
- (a) The  claim/  intimation  should  reach  the  issuing  Bank  on  or before the expiry date.
- (b)  The claim/ intimation should be in strict conformity with the terms of the Guarantee.
- (c) Issuing  Bank  cannot  enquire  into  merits  of  the  claimant  or take  views  on  any  dispute  between  the  applicant  and  the beneficiary.
- (d)  On compliance of terms of the guarantee, payments are to be effected immediately and unconditionally.
