- 3.9.3   Green Channel Status .  DDP/ MoD has formulated a Green Channel Policy  to  promote  ease  of  doing  business.  The  grant  of  Green Channel Status (GCS) to firms provides deemed registration status, waiver of Pre-Dispatch Inspection (PDI) and acceptance of stores under supplier's guarantee/ warranty against contracts concluded by various Procurement Agencies under MoD. The procedure for obtaining  GCS  shall  be  as  per  policy  promulgated  by  DDP  from time to time and available at DGQA web portal

<https://www.dgqadefence.gov.in/greenchannel-policy>.
