
## SCHEDULE OF REQUIREMENTS (SoR)


( The SoR is a technical document and specific to the Project/ Service and is part of the RFP )

- Annexure 1 .  Particulars of Vessel/ Asset on which work is required to be carried out.
- Annexure 2 .    Quantified  Work  Package  {comprehensive  Scope  of  Work  (SoW)}  that  contains break up of individual jobs to be completed including survey, dismantling and inspection, routines and repair to be carried out, consequent repair, trials and comprehensive list of services required such as dry docking, berthing, jetty services (electricity, accommodation, phone, fresh water, fire main), cranes facilities, tugs and pilot charges.
- Annexure 3 .  Quality Inspection Schedules (QIS)/ Quality Assurance Plan (QAP) (minimum and essential parameters that are required to be achieved for the SoW to be deemed as satisfactorily completed).
- Annexure 4 .  Extract of relevant Navy/ Coast Guard orders as applicable pertaining to the scope of work, such as Paint Schemes, Hull Survey and Ratification Procedure, Survey of Anchor Chain Cable, Entry into Confined Spaces and Precautions thereof, etc. Additionally, requirements for Gas Free & Man Entry Certificates, Fire Sentries, Administrative Support to OEMs, Pumping-out Facilities & Removal of Debris/ Waste Material should be clearly specified as applicable.
