- (f) May  examine  cases  covered  by  para  13  above  relating  to  manufacture  under licence/  technology  transfer  agreements  with  a  view  to  satisfying  itself  that adequate mechanisms exist for enforcement of such agreements and for attaining the underlying objective of progressive indigenisation.
- (g) May consider any other issue relating to this Order which may arise.
- Removal  of  Difficulties .    Ministries/  Departments  and  the  Boards  of  Directors  of Government  companies  may  issue  such  clarifications  and  instructions  as  may  be necessary for the removal of any difficulties arising in the implementation of this Order.
- Ministries having Existing Policies .  Where any Ministry or Department has its own policy for preference to local content approved by the Cabinet after 01 Jan 15, such policies shall prevail over the provisions of this Order. All other existing orders on preference to local content shall be reviewed by the Nodal Ministries and revised as needed to conform to this Order, within two months of the issue of this Order.
- Transitional Provision .    This  Order shall not apply to any tender or procurement for which notice inviting tender or other form of procurement solicitation has been issued before the issue of this Order.
