## [Ministry of Finance DoE O.M. No. F No. 4(4)/E.Coord/2015, dated 05.01.2016]


Sub:Instructions for processing foreign visits of officers of the Government of India for approval of Screening Committee of Secretaries (SCoS).


In order to regulate foreign visits of Government of India officers and delegations and to make these visits more effective, the existing sets of guidelines/ instructions  on  the  subject  have  been  comprehensively  reviewed.  The  revised guidelines, as follows, are hereby circulated for strict compliance by all Ministries/ Departments:-

- Ministries/Departments shall upload the data related to foreign visits on the online Foreign Visit Management System (FVMS) which has been developed and can be accessed at the URL notified at the Department of Expenditure website. Each Ministry/ Department has been provided with an user ID and Password for this purpose.
