DELEGATION OF FINANCIAL POWERS RULES, 2024
MINISTRY OF FINANCE
DEPARTMENT OF EXPENDITURE


'The Committee also expect the Financial Advisors of all the Ministries/De -partments to ensure that no violation occur in implementation of the revised limits  for  'New  Service'/'New Instrument of Service'. It goes without saying that any slackness in complying with the same may be strictly dealt with.'

- Department of Expenditure, Ministry of Finance, will issue further orders related to the amendments in provisions of Annexure I to Appendix 3 of the General Financial Rules 2017 consequent to the extant revision in the financial limits of 'New Service'/ 'New Instrument of Service'.
- This issues with the approval of the Finance Secretary and Secretary Expenditure.
- Hindi version will follow.
